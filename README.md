🎯 1. KPI Section (Summary Metrics)

These KPIs are displayed as top cards and provide a quick snapshot of overall performance.

📊 Business Performance KPIs

Total Bookings (250)
→ Total number of flight bookings made.
→ Helps measure overall system usage and demand.

Total Revenue (645K)
→ Total income generated from ticket sales.
→ Indicates business profitability and growth.

Average Ticket Price (2.58K)
→ Average cost per ticket across all bookings.
→ Useful for pricing strategy and revenue optimization.

👥 Customer KPI

Average Passenger Age (45.60)
→ Average age of all passengers.
→ Helps identify the primary customer segment.

✈️ Operational KPIs

Total Distance Traveled (1.89M km)
→ Total kilometers covered by all flights.
→ Indicates operational scale and travel volume.

Average Flight Duration (11.04 hrs)
→ Average time per flight.
→ Helps analyze route length and efficiency.

📈 2. Chart Section (Detailed Analysis)

Charts provide deep insights and comparisons that KPIs alone cannot show.

🏢 Airline Performance Analysis

Chart Type: Cluster Bar Chart

Metric: Sum of Ticket Price by Airline

Details:
Compares revenue generated by each airline (B Airways, S Airlines, etc.)

Insight:
Helps identify top-performing airlines and business partnerships.

🌆 Route / City Analysis

Chart Type: Stacked Column Chart

Metric: Sum of Ticket Price by Departure City

Details:
Shows which cities contribute the most revenue (Mumbai, London, Delhi, etc.)

Insight:
Identifies high-demand routes and profitable locations.

💺 Travel Class Analysis

Chart Type: Funnel Chart

Metric: Count of Bookings by Travel Class

Details:
Displays booking distribution across Economy, Business, First Class, Premium Economy.

Insight:
Helps understand customer preferences and pricing strategy.

💳 Payment Method – Revenue Analysis

Chart Type: Treemap

Metric: Ticket Price by Payment Method

Details:
Shows revenue contribution from each payment method (Credit Card, UPI, etc.)

Insight:
Identifies most profitable payment channels.

💳 Payment Method – Booking Analysis

Chart Type: Donut Chart

Metric: Number of Bookings by Payment Method

Details:
Displays usage percentage of each payment option.

Insight:
Helps understand customer payment behavior.

👥 Passenger Age Analysis

Chart Type: Histogram

Metric: Ticket Price by Passenger Age (bins)

Details:
Groups passengers into age ranges and shows their contribution.

Insight:
Identifies which age group contributes most to revenue.
